The Story

The night her family died, something else was born.

After witnessing her family murdered by corrupt cops, Vesna discovers a deadly power growing inside her. She turns that curse toward revenge, hunting the men responsible while drawing the attention of demons and other dark forces.

Every step brings her closer to the truth, and closer to becoming something she may not be able to come back from.

Meet the Characters

The Cursed

Vesna

Scrappy, resourceful, and carrying a rage she can barely contain. Her hunt for revenge awakens a power that may consume her.

The Survivors

Moth & Robot Baby

A feral ten year old orphan dressed in black and her toy robot companion. Chaotic, fearless, and fiercely loyal.

The Dead

The Sisters

Vesna's murdered sisters, whose dolls return as manifestations of the dead. Their strange power becomes the weapon behind her revenge.

Chief

A polished political predator who controls the city with a smile, a threat, and blood on his hands.

Beyond Stumble & Fall

The mythology goes deeper.

Stumble & Fall is the first chapter of a larger story. As Vesna's power slips from her grasp, questions about her past and what she really is become impossible to ignore.

Book Two, The Exterminating Angel, expands the conflict into forgotten lives, rival factions, the underworld, and a war over whether Vesna can remain human.
